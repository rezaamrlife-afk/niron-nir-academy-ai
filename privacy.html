Privacy Policy

NIRON — Language Intelligence · Last updated: June 2026

NIRON ("we", "our", "the app") is an AI-powered English language learning application, part of the NIR Academy ecosystem. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your email address and the name you provide for authentication purposes. When you use NIRON's learning features, we may store the content you generate (such as saved analyses and outputs) so you can access them across sessions.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to: provide authentication and account access; save your learning outputs to your personal library; and improve the app's functionality. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

AI Processing

The text you submit for analysis is processed by AI services to generate language learning feedback. This text is used only to provide the requested feature and is not used to identify you personally.

Data Storage

Account data and saved outputs are stored securely using Supabase. We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

Third-Party Services

NIRON uses the following third-party services: Supabase (authentication and data storage), and AI processing services for language analysis. Each operates under its own privacy policy.

Children's Privacy

NIRON is intended for general English language learners. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate consent.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact NIR Academy at: info@niracademy.com
