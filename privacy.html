Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026  ·  Effective immediately

NIRON ("we", "our", "the app") is an AI-powered English language learning application developed by NIR Academy, founded by Dr. Niki Majidi (PhD TEFL). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. About NIRON

NIRON is an adaptive language intelligence system that helps users improve their English through:

  • CEFR-level diagnosis and language analysis
  • Vocabulary learning (Vocabulary Hub)
  • Grammar practice and explanation
  • IELTS preparation support
  • Personalized learning pathways

2. Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the service:

  • Email address — for account creation and login
  • Display name — for personalization
  • User-generated content — saved outputs, vocabulary practice results, grammar analyses, CEFR assessments, and language learning history
We do not collect sensitive personal data such as financial information, government IDs, precise location, or biometric data.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Provide account authentication and secure access
  • Save and display your learning history and outputs across sessions
  • Enable core features (Vocabulary Hub, Grammar practice, IELTS prep, CEFR diagnosis)
  • Improve app performance and user experience

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising or commercial purposes.

4. AI Processing

NIRON uses AI services to generate language learning feedback based on your input.

  • Your text input is processed only to provide the requested learning feature
  • Your data is not used to personally train AI models
  • AI responses are generated in real time and are not stored beyond your session unless you explicitly save them

5. Data Storage & Security

Your account data and saved learning outputs are securely stored using Supabase, a trusted cloud database platform. We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

We retain your data for as long as your account remains active.

6. Account & Data Deletion

You have the right to delete your account and all associated data at any time. To request deletion:

  • Email us at info@niracademy.com with your registered email address and a deletion request
  • We will process your request within 30 days
  • All personal data and learning history will be permanently removed

7. Third-Party Services

NIRON relies on the following trusted third-party services:

  • Supabase — authentication and database storage
  • AI processing services — for generating language learning outputs
  • Google Fonts — for typography (Inter, Orbitron, JetBrains Mono)

Each service operates under its own privacy policy. We do not share personal data with advertisers.

8. Children's Privacy

NIRON is designed for English language learners aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided personal information, we will delete it promptly. Parents or guardians who believe their child has submitted data may contact us at info@niracademy.com.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict processing of your data
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@niracademy.com.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
